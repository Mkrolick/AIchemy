# CLAIM-29 — MetanetX yield estimate

**Status:** PLAUSIBLE (estimate, well-calibrated)

**Claim:** 30,000–80,000 priced metabolites out of the 1.5M MetanetX set.

## Verification anchors

### MetanetX scale

- MetaNetX/MNXref 4.5 (2025–2026 update) reconciles compounds from multiple metabolic resources. Historical sizes: MNXref 3.x ≈ 750K–1M; 4.x reconciliation pipeline drove substantial growth. The "1.5M" figure is consistent with current MNXref (precise count not in search snippets but the order of magnitude checks out).
- Most MetanetX entries are theoretical / predicted metabolites or species-specific intermediates; only a small fraction are commercially available as physical samples.

### Commercial metabolite catalog reality

| Vendor | Approx. metabolite-flavored catalog |
|---|---:|
| Sigma-Aldrich (metabolites + Avanti lipids) | tens of thousands |
| Cayman Chemical | ~25,000 (CLAIM-14) |
| MedChemExpress (endogenous metabolites) | several thousand |
| Santa Cruz / ChemCruz | 175,000 biochemicals (CLAIM-17) but most are antibodies/reagents — metabolite subset is smaller |
| Avanti (lipids only) | ~3,000 (Larodan-comparable scale) |

Combined deduped commercially-available metabolite count across these vendors is plausibly in the 30K–80K range. Reference: HMDB itself has 220,945 metabolite entries but only a fraction are commercially purchasable.

## Assessment

| Aspect | Verdict |
|---|---|
| MetanetX 1.5M scale | ✅ Plausible per MNXref 4.5 |
| 30K–80K commercial subset | ✅ Realistic given Sigma + Cayman + MCE + ChemCruz combined deduped coverage |
| Calibration | Well-calibrated — better than CLAIM-28's USPTO estimate which had an internal contradiction |

## Verdict

**PLAUSIBLE — well-calibrated.** Unlike CLAIM-28, this estimate is realistic and operationally useful. Cayman alone (~25K) plus Sigma's metabolite coverage (tens of thousands) plus MCE/ChemCruz filler comfortably lands in the 30K–80K range after dedup. The report's "tens of thousands = success" framing is honest about the scale gap between MetanetX (1.5M theoretical) and commercial reality (~50K). Strategic conclusion (Sigma + Cayman + MCE as metabolite trio) survives.
